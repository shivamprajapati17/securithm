SECURITHM INVESTOR CONVERSATION GUIDE

How to explain Securithm and convince investors
Based on the Securithm Investor Pitch Deck

1. CORE MESSAGE

Start with the problem:

"Smart contract security is too often a last-step audit problem. Developers need security feedback while they are building, not weeks later when they are ready to launch."

One-line solution:

"Securithm is an AI-powered smart contract security platform that gives developers fast, actionable security feedback during build, test, and release."

The investor should remember:
- The problem is expensive and important.
- Securithm moves security from a final audit into a continuous workflow.
- The business can scale through SaaS, pay-per-scan, and enterprise plans.

2. 60-SECOND PITCH

"Web3 developers are still building with a major security gap.

Security often becomes a last-step gate. Manual audits can take weeks, cost a lot, and arrive after developers have already spent time building and changing their contracts.

That creates a simple problem: teams need security feedback earlier.

Securithm is building an AI-powered smart contract security workflow that scans contracts in minutes, identifies likely risks, prioritises findings by severity, and gives developers clear guidance on what to fix.

Instead of waiting for a manual audit at the end, developers can use Securithm during build, testing, and release.

We are starting with developer workflows across Ethereum, Solana, and major Layer 2 ecosystems, with a product-led approach through community adoption, ecosystem partnerships, and integrations such as VS Code and common build pipelines.

Our business model combines SaaS subscriptions, pay-per-scan usage, and enterprise plans.

We have a live MVP and are raising under $250k in a SAFE pre-seed round to expand the platform, launch the API, broaden chain support, and integrate directly into developer workflows.

The vision is simple: make smart contract security faster, more accessible, and part of the way Web3 software is shipped."

3. 3-5 MINUTE STORY

OPENING

"Imagine a team has spent months building a protocol.

They are close to launch.

Then a security review finds a serious issue.

Now the team has to stop, go back into the code, wait for another review, and potentially delay launch.

That is the gap we are solving."

PROBLEM

"Developers often lack a continuous security feedback loop while writing and changing smart contracts.

Security becomes a final checkpoint rather than a daily development workflow.

Manual audits can take weeks and create high costs.

For smaller teams, that cost can also mean auditing less often.

When vulnerabilities reach production, the consequences can include financial loss, delayed launches, and loss of user trust."

SOLUTION

"Securithm moves security earlier in the development cycle.

A developer uploads or pastes a smart contract.

Securithm runs automated checks and uses AI review for context.

The dashboard groups findings by severity and explains the issue.

Suggested next steps help the developer resolve issues before deployment.

The key shift is:

Build -> finish -> audit -> discover issues -> delay

becomes

Build -> scan -> understand -> fix -> ship"

WHY NOW

"Web3 continues to expand, while security expectations are increasing.

Regulation and institutional participation increase the need for stronger security assurance.

Developers also expect modern software tooling to provide fast feedback.

Security should fit into the developer workflow instead of becoming a separate process at the end."

MARKET ENTRY

"We are not trying to solve every security problem on day one.

Our beachhead is developer workflows in Ethereum, Solana, and major Layer 2 ecosystems.

We start with individual builders and growing teams, then move into protocol and enterprise workflows.

Our go-to-market has three parts:

1. Community-led adoption through hackathons, security clinics, templates, and shareable reports.
2. Ecosystem partnerships with L1/L2 foundations, accelerators, and protocol tooling partners.
3. Developer integrations such as VS Code and build pipelines, making security a natural step before testnet and mainnet releases."

BUSINESS MODEL

"We have three revenue streams.

SaaS subscriptions provide predictable recurring revenue for developers and teams.

Pay-per-scan gives occasional users a low-friction way to start.

Enterprise plans provide deeper workflow support and custom contracts for larger protocols."

COMPETITION

"Teams already use firms such as CertiK, ConsenSys Diligence, and Hacken for audits.

Developers also use tools such as Slither and Mythril for fast checks.

Our position is different.

We focus on making automated security analysis fast, accessible, developer-friendly, and actionable inside the shipping workflow.

We are not asking developers to replace every security process. We are making continuous security feedback easier to use before deployment."

TRACTION

"We currently have a live MVP and are using the pre-seed stage to validate the product with early users and expand the platform."

IMPORTANT:
Only mention specific user counts, revenue, contracts, retention, or detection accuracy if you have verified numbers. Do not invent traction.

THE ASK

"We are raising under $250k in a SAFE pre-seed round.

The planned allocation is:
50% engineering
30% go-to-market
20% audits/legal

The capital gives us 6-12 months to expand the platform, launch the API, broaden chain support, and plug Securithm into developer workflows and release cycles."

CLOSING

"We believe smart contract security should not be something developers think about only before launch.

It should be part of how they build.

Securithm's goal is to make that workflow faster, more accessible, and scalable.

We are looking for investors who understand Web3 infrastructure and want to help us turn security from a final gate into a continuous developer workflow."

4. ANSWERS TO COMMON INVESTOR QUESTIONS

WHY THIS PROBLEM?

"Security feedback can arrive too late. Manual audits can take weeks and cost significant money, while developers need fast feedback as code changes."

WHY NOW?

"Web3 development continues to grow, security expectations are rising, and modern developers expect fast feedback inside their existing workflow."

WHY WILL CUSTOMERS PAY?

Explain the value in terms of saved time, reduced rework, faster releases, and stronger security confidence.

WHY CAN THIS SCALE?

"SaaS, usage-based scanning, enterprise plans, API access, and developer integrations allow the product to serve users from individual builders to larger protocols."

WHY NOT CERTIK?

"CertiK and other audit providers are important parts of the security ecosystem. We are approaching a different point in the workflow. An audit can be a major review before launch, while Securithm is designed to give developers fast feedback while they are building and changing contracts. Deeper audits can still remain part of the process for higher-risk deployments."

WHY AI?

"We do not position AI as a replacement for expert security research or formal audits. Securithm uses automated checks together with AI-assisted review to help explain findings and turn them into actionable next steps."

WHAT IS YOUR MOAT?

"Our long-term moat is becoming deeply embedded in developer workflows through integrations, adoption, ecosystem partnerships, security feedback, and product usability. The goal is to become part of the release process rather than another website developers visit occasionally."

WHO IS YOUR FIRST CUSTOMER?

"We start with indie Web3 developers and small-to-growing teams that need fast security feedback but may not have the budget or time for repeated manual audits. Once the workflow proves valuable, we expand into larger protocol and enterprise teams."

7. INVESTOR CONVERSATION RULES

1. Sell the problem before the product.
2. Do not overload investors with technical details.
3. Use simple examples.
4. Never invent traction or metrics.
5. Do not claim AI makes smart contracts completely safe.
6. Do not attack competitors.
7. Explain exactly why developers will pay.
8. Explain how you acquire the first users.
9. Explain what the funding unlocks.
10. Finish with a clear vision.

8. STRONGEST ONE-LINER

"Securithm turns smart contract security from a final audit checkpoint into a continuous developer workflow."

Securithm brings that feedback into the workflow itself.

We have the MVP.

Now we want to prove adoption, expand the platform, and make security a default part of how smart contracts are shipped.

That is what this pre-seed round will help us build."
